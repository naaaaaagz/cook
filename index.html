Privacy and browser storage

What we use and why

When you create an account, we use your email, an account identifier, password authentication records and verification status to provide and secure your account. Passwords are handled by the authentication provider as secure hashes; Cook does not store readable passwords. We store your kitchen layout, selected language, theme and measurement units to restore your settings across devices. Providing an account email and password is necessary for an account; you can browse recipes without one.

Account and settings processing is necessary to provide the account service you request (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Limited security and abuse-prevention records may be processed under legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)), subject to necessity and balancing. Verification and password-reset emails are service messages, not marketing. No advertising or analytics is added by the account feature.

Browser storage

Cook uses sessionStorage to carry your kitchen, cooking progress and choices between pages in the same tab. When signed in, it also holds your authentication session. The browser normally clears this when the tab session ends, but browser session restoration can retain it. Signing out clears Cook's tab data. The storage-notice dismissal is kept in that tab only. Cook's own scripts do not set cookies. Your hosting or service providers may have separate security processing that must be documented before launch.

OK and the close button on the storage notice only dismiss the notice. They do not authorise analytics, advertising or other optional tracking. Any future non-essential storage requires a separate choice before it starts, with an equally easy refusal and withdrawal.

Other existing features

The current recipe-list speech feature requests an approximate country from country.is using your IP address to choose a speech language. If you use microphone recognition, your browser may send audio to its speech-service provider. Typed ingredient entry is available instead. These providers, their policies and the legal basis for these features must be reviewed before public launch.

Retention and deletion

Account settings are kept while your account is active. You may request deletion using the privacy contact above. Before launch the operator must specify the deletion response process, unverified-account expiry, security-log retention and backup-expiry periods. Deleted data may remain in restricted backups until the documented backup retention period ends.

Your rights

Depending on the applicable conditions, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to legitimate-interest processing. Contact the operator above; requests are normally answered within one month, with any lawful extension explained. You can complain to your supervisory authority, including Hungary's NAIH at naih.hu. Cook does not use account settings for automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.
