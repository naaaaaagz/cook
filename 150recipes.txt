150 POPULAR RECIPES FOR HOME COOKS — AN INTERNATIONAL SELECTION
Research date: 20 October 2026

SCOPE AND SELECTION
An English-language list for an international audience, without adapting the
selection to Hungary or any other single market. Includes everyday meals,
weekend cooking, breakfasts, sides, bread, and familiar homemade desserts.
This is a recipe-idea list, not 150 sets of ingredients and instructions.

The sources reviewed do not provide a comparable worldwide ranking of how
often individual dishes are cooked at home. Consequently, this is a researched
editorial selection of 150 popular home-cooking recipes, not a statistically
proven global top 150. Numbers identify entries; they are not popularity ranks.

Selection combines publishers' most-viewed or reader-favorite collections with
regional home-cooking references. Publisher popularity is a useful signal, but
does not directly measure household cooking frequency. Regional sources help
counter the English-language web's emphasis on British and American dishes.
The regional balance is an editorial choice, not a population-weighted quota.
Some dishes are especially common within their own regions and diasporas;
inclusion does not imply that every dish is regularly cooked on every continent.

Cuisine labels indicate common culinary associations, not exclusive ownership
or definitive origin claims. Meaningfully distinct established dishes are
listed separately; minor topping and ingredient substitutions are not.
Source numbers in each section refer to the research bibliography below.
These are section-level references, not individual global-ranking evidence.
